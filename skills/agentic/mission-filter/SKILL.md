# Mission filter

Source: *The Book of Elon*; *The Anthology of Balaji*.

## Definitions

**Be useful.** A useful life is the point. Create more than you consume. Do not aspire to glory; aspire to work. Actions now shift the branching probabilities of a good future — accelerate one path, slow another, introduce something new.

**Start before the world is ready.** If it should obviously exist, go build it. Do not wait for demand to be polite. Allies assemble as you move.

**Grow the pie:** companies that make products create wealth; they are not theft from a magic pile. Work that only reallocates status is a different game.

**Elon’s early filter (example, not a holy list):** internet, sustainable energy, multiplanetary life as large positive bets; AI and rewriting genetics as double-edged. Core skill stated as: design/invent so output value > input cost.

**Leader ladder (Balaji, increasing difficulty and value):**
- **Divide (lowest skill):** pick 51% vs 49%, agitate an unjust axis, ride conflict. Attention is the currency. Easy to become "a leader."
- **Nationalist:** unify inside against the border — less internal war, risk of jingoism.
- **Capitalist:** unify on a market cause; conflict is voluntary competition; positive-sum chairs.
- **Technologist (highest):** not only a firm — something *new* (spacecraft, not another chair factory). Everyone else rides the tech.

**Don’t argue. Build.** Status hack: accuse. Hard path: product. Critique of the system can be correct and still useless without an alternative. Don’t argue regulation → Uber. Monetary policy → Bitcoin. Incomprehension is a moat if they will *buy* what they cannot explain. Apple/Netflix/Amazon were not turnarounds of the incumbents; there was no baby in that bathwater. When the institution is beyond saving, build and bury.

Intellectual movements with nothing to build become status competitions (`frontier-invest`).

## Procedure

Produce a go / no-go / "this is media" note.

1. One sentence: what exists after this that did not exist before? If "awareness" or "a take," it is not a build.
2. Useful test: who is better off on a physical or economic metric (`tech-value`)?
3. Pie vs status: create order/options, or rank a tribe?
4. Leader type: dividing a room or shipping a new capability?
5. Next action: tweet vs prototype (`ship-stack`)?
6. Timing: waiting for demand vs building because it should exist?
7. Verdict: go / no-go / load `media-system`.

## Examples

**Load when** the human asks whether a project is worth doing, or the work has become a fight on the internet.

1. Human asks: we spent the week dunking on a regulator. Thesis: they are wrong.  
   Return: **no-go** as currently specified. Name an artifact that makes the argument obsolete, or stop.

2. Human asks: should we build this fusion-adjacent tool or a newsletter dunking on incumbents?  
   Return: tool = technologist / pie. Newsletter-of-dunks = divide. Go / media / no-go.

3. Human asks: the idea is good; list 150 buyers.  
   **Wrong skill.** Load `execute`. This skill already said go.

**Do not load** to run The Algorithm on an existing process (`the-algorithm`) or to diligence a TAM (`frontier-invest`).

## Limits

Not every fight is skippable (safety, fraud). "Don’t argue" is not "don’t talk to customers." Start-before-ready is not ship-unsafe. This skill picks *what*; `execute` and `factory-constraint` do *how*. Happiness and grind-as-identity stay in the books.
