# Media system

Source: *The Anthology of Balaji*.

## Definitions

**Media:** how humans know what to do. Code scripts machines; media scripts humans. You think what you see. Sources are upstream of “you.”

**Shimmery mirror:** reality far side, what you read near side, the outlet in the middle. Detect the middle, do not treat the near side as the world.

**Legacy / pageview media:** corporations. Interests ≠ yours. Act accordingly. Incentive: if it enrages, it engages; if it bleeds, it leads. Conflict is the product. A **scissor statement** is obviously true to one tribe and obviously false to the other — selected because it is enraging.

**Infotainment vs news-you-can-use:** war reporting and celebrity gossip sit on the *same* end (spectacle). The other end is tutorials, weather, prices — you confirm and apply each line (code on the left, terminal on the right). If you will not verify and act, it is diet junk.

**Clickbait distortion:** headline almost unbelievable, thin tether to fact, at the boundary of a lie. Sold as entertainment. Journalists often do not control their own headlines.

**Hierarchy in language:** you harass / they hold accountable; you doxx / they investigate; hacked vs documents obtained. Same act, embedded rank.

**Go direct:** quotes and interviews are free content you give a competitor. Founders who do not own distribution are not doing the job — like having no website. Own a media corporation or be owned by one.

**Standing media:** a class with special powers, separate from the people — like a standing army. Citizen journalists are the check. Vice/Vox/BuzzFeed still collapsed into the same Brooklyn social graph. A new typical media *company* is not the answer.

**Pre-narrative / event feed:** sports scores, ticks, on-chain logs, timestamps. Narrative is wrapping. **Oracle** = signed sensor/fact with a track record. **Advocate** = human who selects which facts to tell. Selection (and omission) is the editorial act; make it visible.

**Important vs news:** what is important often is not new; what is new often is not important.

**Dollowers:** paying, engaged supporters — scarce. Followers are cheap commenters. Virtue signaling vs **value signaling** (funded or created).

**Founding influencer:** same rank as founding engineer. Content is write-access to other people’s heads; computer literacy is write-access to machines.

**Change the world (two steps):** (1) discover true facts (2) acquire sufficient distribution. Neither alone is enough.

## Detect

1. Name the system: outlet, feed, Slack rumor, your own launch post, a startup “content strategy.”
2. Business model: pageviews / prestige / ads / subscriptions / equity in outcomes / prediction skin. Who pays, for what action?
3. Spectrum: infotainment or news-you-can-use? Would a competent reader confirm and apply every line?
4. Scissors and absences: what topics are optimized to split? What owners, incentives, and conflicts are *not* covered? Editorial judgment is louder in absence.
5. Distortion: headline vs body vs primary table (`truth-types`: ask for the table). 10,000× magnification of fitting stories vs silence on the rest is the usual net.
6. Hierarchy tricks: dual-class OK for them, not for you; leak-and-puff vs non-cooperators get attacked — invert the coverage.
7. Novelty slot machine: 30 random links, no compounding direction. Overconsuming novelty, underconsuming purpose.
8. Verdict: **spectacle** / **status** / **usable** / **oracle-layer**. If spectacle, do not treat it as a truth machine.

## Build

Do not found another Brooklyn newsroom. Build an alternative.

1. **Intent:** what measurable variable does the reader move (truth, health, wealth, a specific skill, a portfolio, a factory metric)? Dashboards beat newspapers. Maximum value, minimum time.
2. **Spectrum position:** tutorial, price, sensor, research report for an intellectual vertical (not geography) — e.g. live 3D-print industry map a founder or investor can act on. Headline-alone must be *insufficient*.
3. **Fact layer:** event feeds, citations, revision history, forkable sources (GitHub-of-facts). Separate oracle vs advocate. List what was omitted.
4. **Writer incentives:** pay for correctness and outcomes, not pageviews. Prediction markets / disclosed bets / track record API. Optional: equity if the piece causes a company. Ratings for articles, not only videos.
5. **Distribution:** go direct. Company media arm. Citizen journalists, full-stack writers (article → video → film). Dollowers, not follower vanity. Crypto payments if you need cross-border proof of support.
6. **Sentiment as the non-zero-sum metric** when the job is to move a technology (Mars, fusion): measure keyword sentiment, produce until it swings. Clicks and awards are legacy scores.
7. **Hygiene:** popularity ≠ independent confirmation. Downrank scissors if the goal is truth (this costs engagement; say so). Block establishment feed if the goal is peer-to-peer (diet, not a product feature everyone must ship).
8. **Career / founding:** every citizen a journalist; every company a media company; media scales. Tech people must write, report, publish, direct — not only tweet. Founding influencer + founding engineer.

Math check for paid media: differential profit from *acting* on the piece must routinely beat the cost of the piece. Entertainment fails that test on purpose.

## Examples

**Load when** the human asks how to get press, whether to give a quote, or how to build a feed/publication.

1. Human asks: we want thought leadership in TechCrunch.  
   Return: you are the *subject*; headline is not yours. Build: research notes, changelog + table, founder writing with a prediction log. Metric = readers ship or invest, not impressions.

2. Human asks: audit this launch post vs this rage-bait thread we were going to quote.  
   Return: infotainment vs news-you-can-use. Scissors in the thread. Go-direct URL as the source. Do not donate the quote.

3. Human asks: is this churn number true?  
   **Wrong skill** for the claim type. Load `truth-types`. Then use this skill if they want a better information product.

**Do not load** to pick ads vs virality (`distribution`) unless the work is the media system itself.

## Limits

Fun is allowed; a diet of only cookies is not. Serendipity has some value; it is overconsumed. Oracles can lie — compare sensors, publish track records. Sentiment campaigns are activism; label them. This skill does not tell you to harass journalists; it tells you to stop confusing their P&L with a public good. Pair still-untyped claims with `truth-types`.
